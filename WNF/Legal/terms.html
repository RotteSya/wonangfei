WNF Terms of Use

Last updated: May 18, 2026.

WNF is a local-first wage tracking utility. It is not financial, tax, employment, or legal advice.

Premium Purchase

Premium is a one-time non-consumable in-app purchase processed by Apple. Pricing and availability are shown by the App Store before purchase. Restore Purchases is available in the app.

Refunds

Refund requests are handled by Apple. The app may offer an in-app refund request entry when a valid purchase is found.

Data

Wage settings and history are stored locally on the device unless you explicitly export them.

Contact

For support, contact the developer through the App Store support channel.
