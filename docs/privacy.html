WNF Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 18, 2026.

Local Data

Salary, schedule, privacy settings, theme choices, and daily wage records are stored on your device. They are not uploaded by WNF.

Payments

Version 1 does not offer paid features, subscriptions, or in-app purchases. WNF does not collect payment card information.

Exports

If you export history, the generated CSV and JSON files contain full wage amounts and work time data. You control where those files are shared.

Diagnostics

WNF uses on-device system logging for local diagnostics. Logs are not sent to a third-party analytics service in version 1.

Tracking

WNF does not track you across apps or websites for advertising.
