WNF Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026.

Local Data

Salary, schedule, privacy settings, and daily wage records are stored on your device. They are not uploaded by WNF.

Payments

The app currently does not offer paid features, subscriptions, or in-app purchases. WNF does not collect payment card information.

Sharing

WNF does not export wage history as CSV or JSON files. You can share a daily report or settlement voucher as an image. That image may include the amounts and times you chose to show. You control where it is saved or sent. WNF does not upload the image.

Diagnostics

WNF uses on-device system logging for local diagnostics. The app currently does not send logs to a third-party analytics service.

Tracking

WNF does not track you across apps or websites for advertising.
