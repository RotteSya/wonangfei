WNF Terms of Use

WNF is a local-first wage tracking utility. It is not financial, tax, employment, or legal advice.

No Paid Features

Version 1 does not offer paid features, subscriptions, or in-app purchases.

Data

Wage settings and history are stored only on your device. WNF does not upload them. Sharing a report image is optional and stays under your control.
